01 / ABOUT MMA

WHAT IS MMA?

Mixed Martial Arts is a combat sport that combines techniques from different martial arts disciplines.

MMA fighters can combine striking, wrestling, grappling and submission techniques. The sport requires physical preparation, technical skill, strategy and discipline.

Fighters may use techniques from boxing, Muay Thai, Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, wrestling, judo, karate and other martial arts.
